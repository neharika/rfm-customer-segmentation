# Customer Segment Definitions

## 1. Overview

The analysis identified five actionable customer segments from 6,052 customers using Recency, Frequency and Monetary (RFM) scoring. Segmentation combines purchasing behaviour with business rules to distinguish high-value customers, loyal buyers, emerging opportunities and inactive customers.

Revenue is measured as net monetary value after successful refunds.

## 3. Segment Definitions and Campaign Recommendations

### Champions

**Definition:** Customers with R≥4, F≥4 and M≥4.

Champions represent 12.77% of customers but contribute 46.39% of net revenue. They average 11.90 purchases and have the highest average monetary value ($89,632.93).

**Campaign:** Prioritize retention through VIP rewards, early product access, exclusive offers and personalized recommendations. Protect this segment from churn because it contributes nearly half of total revenue.

### Loyal Customers

**Definition:** Customers with R≥3 and F≥4 who do not qualify as Champions.

This segment contains 219 customers (3.62%) and contributes 6.81% of revenue. Customers average 6.78 purchases, indicating established repeat-purchase behaviour, although their recency and spending levels are below those of Champions.

**Campaign:** Encourage continued engagement through loyalty benefits, personalized product recommendations and purchase milestones. Identify customers with increasing spending potential and nurture them towards the Champions segment.

### Emerging Customers

**Definition:** Customers who do not meet the rules for the other four segments.

This is the largest segment, representing 44.55% of customers and 25.99% of revenue. Average purchase frequency is 1.91 orders.

Further analysis shows two distinct behaviours: one-time buyers account for 54.12% of this segment but contribute only 28.03% of its revenue. Repeat buyers account for 45.88% of customers and generate 71.97% of segment revenue.

**Campaign:** Encourage second purchases among one-time buyers through complementary-product recommendations and targeted incentives. For repeat buyers, use bundles, personalized offers and loyalty benefits to increase purchase frequency.

### At Risk

**Definition:** Customers with R≤2 and either F≥3 or M≥3.

These 1,141 customers represent 18.85% of the base and contribute 18.02% of revenue. Their average recency is 67.28 days. Despite reduced recent activity, their historical frequency or spending indicates potential value worth recovering.

**Campaign:** Prioritize targeted win-back campaigns using personalized offers, reminders and product recommendations. Focus recovery spending on customers with stronger historical purchase frequency and monetary value.
